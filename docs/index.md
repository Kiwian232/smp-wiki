# Welcome to Pineapple SMP !!

Pineapple SMP (PSMP) is the [third server](history.md) in a line of related Minecraft SMPs.

PSMP is run by [Kiwian (KiwianDoesThings)](figures.md#kiwian).<br>
This wiki was made by Kiwian, with the help of members of the PSMP, and was suggested by [Nyx (Nyxophene)](figures.md#nyx)

This wiki should cover all important aspects of the PSMP, as well as link to all [connected pages](links.md).

Every season, all wiki pages that relate to season-specific information will be archived, and new ones will be made. You will be able to view page archives.
