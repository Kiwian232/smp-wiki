### BSMP-1

BSMP was the first season of BSMP. Whenever BSMP without a -season suffix is mentioned, you should assume it is referring to **this season** of BSMP. This season started on 3/17/2025 and ended on 6/21/2025. This server was originally developed in partnership with Hailey's server, as Redy was an admin in that server.

Some 17+ members were sharing explicit/NSFW photos, and this resulted in Cibles moderators banning all members of the group chat from the Cibles server. Hailey, one of the 17+ members of the group chat, was also banned of course. As they were also very popular, they created a new chatting server. This is where I come in, too. After a few weeks, Redy created a Minecraft SMP and a seperate Discord server to go along with it, and partnered the server with Hailey's. I also joined this SMP/Discord. BSMP was a pretty normal SMP/Server to be completely honest. When staff (media manager) applications came around, I was the only one to sign up, and eventually became the sole moderator of BSMP. On a "side" note: Hailey was later exposed for something... I don't remember exactly what but it was definitely sex-related so probably pedophilia. After that, their server got deleted.   

After a 2-3 month season, Redy decided to reset the server for a new season. However, things didn't go so well. They archived the old BSMP Discord and created an entirely new one. As well, they experimented a LOT with the SMP. They tried a ton of different things, most of which had negative impacts and lots of backlash.
