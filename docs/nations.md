# Nations

This is a list of nations (in order of creation date) in PSMP only. For nations in past servers, check out [history](history.md).

### Clemonstide

Clemonstide is run by [Kiwian](figures.md#kiwian) and [Hoki](figures.md#hoki). Clemonstide has nine members including:

 - Madeline
 - Cambrian
 - Melodii
 - HorrorCrow
 - Ren
 - Angel
 - Press

Clemonstide is currently closed as it exceeds the 8 member limit.

Clemonstide is the largest lit area in [PSMP](history.md#pineapple-smp), with the entire nation being almost entirely lit.
