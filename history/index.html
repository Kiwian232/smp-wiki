History

PSMP is only the third server in a line of servers. The history of these servers is long, complicated, and not best explained by me. But here is a best-effort description of it. (Most of the history will be focused on BSMP-PSMP, as I was not apart of CSMP.)

Cibles SMP

Cibles SMP (CSMP) was a Minecraft SMP run by YouTuber CiblesGD. CSMP had 5 seasons.
Many members and prominent figures of BSMP/PSMP came from CSMP.

Blueberry SMP

Blueberry SMP (BSMP) was a Minecraft SMP run by user Redy2794 (Redy). BSMP had 1 successful season. A second season was attempted, but would inevitably fail due to a variety of factors.

Pineapple SMP

Pineapple SMP (PSMP) is the current Minecraft SMP run by yours truly, Kiwian (KiwianDoesThings). PSMP is currently in its first and so-far only season.

Server Lineage

This section will explain how each server led to the creation of the next, and how some of these motherfuckers were so determined to play Minecraft that they somehow kept the server line alive.

Starting is CSMP. CSMP had a lot of drama. Glossing over some major points, CSMP had a banwave where a lot of very prominent members of the community were banned. This wave was the result of a group chat being discovered by Cibles moderators. In this group chat were a lot of 17+ members, as well as Redy, who was currently around 13-14. Some 17+ members were sharing explicit/NSFW photos, and this resulted in Cibles moderators banning all members of the group chat from the Cibles server. Redy, one of the members in this group chat and server, was a very prominent figure in CSMP, and the server largely died after she was banned.

Hailey, one of the 17+ members of the group chat, was also banned of course. As they were also very popular, they created a new chatting server. This is where I come in, too. I was friends with Hailey back in CSMP and joined Hailey's server when it was created. Redy also joined and became a moderator on Hailey's server. After a few weeks, Redy created a Minecraft SMP and a seperate Discord server to go along with it, and partnered the server with Hailey's. I also joined this SMP/Discord. BSMP was a pretty normal SMP/Server to be completely honest. When staff (media manager) applications came around, I was the only one to sign up, and eventually became the sole moderator of BSMP. On a "side" note: Hailey was later exposed for something... I don't remember exactly what but it was definitely sex-related so probably pedophilia. After that, their server got deleted.

After a 2-3 month season, Redy decided to reset the server for a new season. However, things didn't go so well. They archived the old BSMP Discord and created an entirely new one. As well, they experimented a LOT with the SMP. They tried a ton of different things, most of which had negative impacts and lots of backlash. As well, they did these changes live with people, instead of testing stuff themselves and releasing the server to the public when it was finished. These included:

  • Moving from paper to forge to allow for modding. This made the SMP unplayably laggy and removed Bedrock support. This was reverted.
  • Adding tons of game-changing plugins/datapacks that were annoying, broken, and took away from the vanilla experience. This was not reverted before BSMP2's cancellation.
  • Polling almost every change. This was done to try to reduce backlash so only changes people liked would happen, but instead it caused insane amounts of arguing and controversy.
  • Letting people poll for hardcore mode. This alone was really bad because tons of people voted for this, but (as should have been obvious) almost everyone (including those who voted for it) hated the change.

The stress from how poorly the server was going and everybody getting angry at polls not going their way caused Redy to eventually shut down BSMP entirely. Afterwards, the BSMP2 Discord turned into Blueberries Inc., a chatting server run by Redy. This server had Kiwian and Grillby as mods, from BSMP1, as well as some newly elected moderators, those being: Cass, Nyx, and Seb. Blueberries Inc. worked well for a while, but after a few weeks, Redy started going offline for long periods of time. At some point, Redy didn't interact in Blueberries Inc. for around a month (although we now know she had a good amount of communications with Grillby, and she did text me once to say happy birthday). Eventually, they came back online and said hi to the server. Roughly 5-10 minutes later from my recolletion, she made an announcement giving up her ownership of the server, saying that she was giving it to one of the BITCHASS exes who was in the server. Almost immediately after granting him power, he and his friends rampaged the server, along with Redy. That group threw lots of hateful words at a variety of members, including telling people to commit s-ic-de. As much as I hate to say it, the hateful messages were mostly led by Redy. After another few minutes, the new group in power started banning every other member from the server. It was shortly after I realized what they were doing that I quickly created a new Discord (now PSMP) and started frantically inviting as many members to it as I could.

Finally, PSMP arose.
