    Rules

    Minecraft rules for PSMP include:

    • No griefing / stealing. 2 Strikes.
    • Don't kill people repeatedly if they ask you to stop please. 1 strike.
    • No raid farms. 3 strikes.
    • Obviously absolutely no hacking or cheating. Instant ban.
    • No banned mods, those being:
      • Movement mods (excluding invmove)
      • Hacked clients
      • X-ray mods
      • Auto clickers
      • 2 strikes
    • All rules that apply to the discord apply to the Minecraft server as well. Same amount of strikes as the rule's warns.

    Bans:
    4 warns = Ban for the Discord server. You may appeal 1 ban.
    4 strikes = Ban for the Minecraft server. You may not appeal a Minecraft ban.

    Nations

    Nations are officially recognized groups of people in the server. To create a nation, you need to have a name for your nation, a leader (optionally co-leader), a banner, and at least 3 members. Approved nations, go in the nation list. Nation leaders are able to vote on certain server decisions, and you will be given a category in the Discord for your nation. Your nation will also receive a warp to it via /warp.

    To see a list of nations, check out nations
