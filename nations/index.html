Nations

This is a list of nations (in order of creation date) in PSMP only. For nations in past servers, check out history.

Clemonstide

Clemonstide is run by Kiwian and Hoki. Clemonstide has nine members including:

  • Cass
  • Kesari
  • Melodii
  • HorrorCrow
  • Ren
  • Angel
  • Press

Clemonstide is currently closed as it exceeds the 8 member limit.

Clemonstide is the largest lit area in PSMP, with the entire nation being almost entirely lit.
